# Visual Findings from DET v7 Beyond Consciousness Simulations

The generated time-series figure shows a clear separation among the layers proposed in the attached paper. The `agency_only` condition retains high agency in the core but remains at near-zero selfhood because coherence, pointer stability, and host fitness remain too weak. The `identity_without_observer` condition preserves a stable regime identity while suppressing observer/selfhood readout, supporting the claim that **identity persistence is not equivalent to observerhood**.

The `observer_development` and `boundary_participation` scenarios show delayed selfhood onset near step 783, even though agency is high from the beginning. Selfhood rises only after coherence increases, q decreases, reciprocity and host fitness improve, and developmental maturity accumulates. This supports a DET-native version of the paper’s claim that observerhood is emergent from mature boundary coherence rather than identical to primitive agency.

The boundary-participation proxy remains zero in all cases except the declared `boundary_participation` condition, where a local recovery/healing analogue is applied after maturity. Its magnitude remains small, which is scientifically useful: it prevents the model from treating spirit/higher-boundary participation as a generic synonym for selfhood. The proxy requires both selfhood and an additional local boundary-compatible signal.

The summary bar chart emphasizes the main falsifier: agency and stable identity can be present without observerhood, while observerhood can emerge without automatically producing the higher-boundary proxy. This supports a layered rather than collapsed interpretation of Agency → Identity → Observer → Higher Boundary Participation.
