## 1. Purpose and scientific stance

This document formalizes the idea that an **ever-growing utopic regime** may be operating concurrently with the present world. The prior repository document on a “coexisting kingdom” framed this as a high-coherence regime that is not merely future but coexisting with ordinary embodied reality.[1] The v7-safe formulation must be more restrictive. A concurrently operating utopic regime may be modeled as a **boundary regime** only if it respects DET v7’s strict locality, Agency-First invariance, and declared boundary-operator rules.[2]

The purpose is not to prove a theological claim. The purpose is to convert the claim into a mathematical research object with falsifiers. The model should preserve the intuition that a higher-coherence order may be available now, while preventing it from becoming a hidden global force that can explain anything.

> **Boundary-regime definition:** A utopic regime \(\mathcal{K}\) is a high-coherence, low-damage, recovery-enabling regime that may participate in present dynamics only through declared local boundary channels and never through direct agency override or nonlocal state injection.

## 2. Formal object

Let the ordinary embodied regime be \(\Omega\subset V\). Let the utopic regime be \(\mathcal{K}\subset V_K\), which may be represented as a high-coherence boundary-adjacent region, a structured boundary condition, or a separate but locally coupled subgraph. Its state summary is

\[
X_{\mathcal{K}}(k)=\{F_{\mathcal{K}},q_{\mathcal{K}},a_{\mathcal{K}},\sigma_{\mathcal{K}},P_{\mathcal{K}},C_{\mathcal{K}},\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{K}}\}.
\]

The phrase “utopic” should not mean mathematically perfect at every node. It should mean that \(\mathcal{K}\) has a stable high-coherence attractor and strong lawful recovery capacity:

\[
\bar C_{\mathcal{K}}\rightarrow 1,
\qquad
\Pi_{damage}(\mathcal{K})\rightarrow 0,
\qquad
\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{K}}\text{ remains identity-preserving,}
\qquad
\mathcal{B}_{\mathcal{K}}>\Theta_B.
\]

Here \(\Pi_{damage}\) is a readout decomposition over v7’s unified canonical `q`, not a replacement for it.[2]

| Prior language | v7-safe replacement |
|---|---|
| “Parallel realm” | Boundary-adjacent or separately coupled regime. |
| “Already present future” | Current availability of lawful coupling channels. |
| “Perfect coherence” | High-coherence attractor with explicit thresholds. |
| “Grace cascades into the world” | Local boundary operators acting through `F`, `C`, or `q`. |
| “Merging realities” | Increasing density and strength of lawful coupling bridges. |

## 3. Present operation criterion

The statement that \(\mathcal{K}\) is operating now must be translated into a present coupling criterion:

\[
\mathcal{K}\text{ operates now for }i\in\Omega
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\;\kappa_{iK}(k)>0
\]

where \(\kappa_{iK}\) is a declared local or boundary-local coupling channel active at the current update. Without \(\kappa_{iK}\), \(\mathcal{K}\) may remain an interpretive global structure, but it is not an active causal variable in the local simulation.

A suitable coupling vector is

\[
\kappa_{iK}=\{\kappa^G_{iK},\kappa^H_{iK},\kappa^J_{iK},\kappa^C_{iK},\kappa^{ptr}_{iK}\},
\]

where the channels correspond to local resource support, healing of coherence bonds, Jubilee-like debt recovery, coherence bridge formation, and pointer/record compatibility.

| Channel | Target | Canonical compatibility |
|---|---|---|
| \(\kappa^G\) Grace-like resource support | `F_i` | Compatible if local, agency-gated, and no hidden global normalization. |
| \(\kappa^H\) Healing | Bond coherence `C_ij` | Compatible if local and bond-mediated. |
| \(\kappa^J\) Jubilee | Structural burden `q_i` | Compatible if energy-coupled and local. |
| \(\kappa^C\) Coherence bridge | Interface bond topology | Requires declared interface edges. |
| \(\kappa^{ptr}\) Record alignment | Pointer stability / identity readout | Readout-only unless promoted as a declared operator. |

## 4. Ever-growing regime as expanding boundary accessibility

The “ever-growing” property should not mean that every node is forced into perfection. It should mean that the accessible recovery/coupling capacity of \(\mathcal{K}\) increases over update history:

\[
\frac{d}{dk}\,\mathcal{A}_{access}(\mathcal{K},\Omega)\ge0
\quad\text{in expectation,}
\]

where

\[
\mathcal{A}_{access}(\mathcal{K},\Omega)=
\sum_{i\in\Omega}\left(
 w_G\kappa^G_{iK}+w_H\kappa^H_{iK}+w_J\kappa^J_{iK}+w_C\kappa^C_{iK}+w_P\kappa^{ptr}_{iK}
\right).
\]

This is an important weakening. It permits a growing utopic regime while preserving local histories, local resistance, and non-monotonic embodied trajectories. The regime grows by increasing lawful accessibility, not by coercively rewriting the world.

| Growth interpretation | Scientific status |
|---|---|
| More local channels become available. | Testable in simulations. |
| Coupling strength increases where compatibility exists. | Testable with parameter sweeps. |
| Recovery becomes statistically more likely. | Testable as a long-run distributional shift. |
| Every node improves monotonically. | Too strong and likely false under DET locality. |
| All resistance is impossible. | Violates non-coercive agency framing. |

## 5. Interaction with agency and consent

A utopic regime becomes coercive if it writes directly to agency. DET v7 forbids direct boundary writes to `a`.[2] Therefore, the core constraint is

\[
\frac{\partial a_i}{\partial \mathcal{K}}\bigg|_{direct}=0.
\]

Any apparent transformation of agency must occur indirectly through presence, coherence, reduced structural burden, or improved host conditions:

\[
\mathcal{K}\rightarrow (F_i,C_{ij},q_i,H_i,P_i)\rightarrow \Delta a_i.
\]

The model can include a consent or openness readout, but it should be defined as local compatibility rather than a mystical override:

\[
O_{iK}=a_i^{r_a}C_{iK}^{r_C}(1-q_i)^{r_q}R_i^{r_R}M_i^{r_M}.
\]

Higher \(O_{iK}\) permits stronger lawful coupling. Lower \(O_{iK}\) reduces coupling without deleting agency.

## 6. Observation without interference

Prior coexisting-kingdom language suggests that high-coherence observers might perceive lower-coherence life patterns as unified wholes.[1] A v7-safe version should distinguish **readout access** from **causal intervention**:

\[
\mathrm{Observe}_{\mathcal{K}}(\Omega)=\mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{R}_\Omega)
\]

where \(\mathcal{Q}\) is a read-only query over the identity record. By itself,

\[
\mathrm{Observe}_{\mathcal{K}}(\Omega)\not\Rightarrow \Delta X_\Omega.
\]

Causal change requires an additional declared operator:

\[
\mathrm{Act}_{\mathcal{K}\to\Omega} = \mathrm{Observe}_{\mathcal{K}}(\Omega)+\kappa_{iK}+\mathcal{O}_{boundary}.
\]

This distinction allows the paper to discuss “being known” by a higher boundary without turning observation into nonlocal manipulation.

## 7. Merging as interface densification

“Merging worlds” should be formalized as increasing interface density and compatibility, not as collapse of locality:

\[
\rho_{interface}(k)=\frac{|E_{\Omega K}(k)|}{|V_\Omega|+|V_K|},
\qquad
\bar C_{interface}(k)=\frac{1}{|E_{\Omega K}|}\sum_{(i,K)\in E_{\Omega K}}C_{iK}.
\]

A merge trajectory exists when

\[
\rho_{interface}(k)\uparrow,
\qquad
\bar C_{interface}(k)\uparrow,
\qquad
\Pi_{damage,\Omega}(k)\downarrow\text{ in coupled regions,}
\]

while disconnected regions remain unaffected except through newly formed lawful interfaces.

| Merge phase | DET signature | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| No coupling | \(\rho_{interface}=0\) | Utopic regime is not locally causal for \(\Omega\). |
| Sparse contact | Few high-coherence interface edges | Local healing or resource support possible. |
| Bridge formation | Increasing \(E_{\Omega K}\) and \(C_{interface}\) | Coherence-compatible participation grows. |
| Local phase transition | Host region sustains high \(C\), low damage readout, and stable identity | Resurrection or utopic embodiment analogue. |
| Non-coercive expansion | Access grows without direct agency writes | Utopic regime grows lawfully. |

## 10. Recommended language for the paper

The paper should avoid saying that a perfected world simply overwrites the present. A stronger and more scientific statement is:

> A higher-coherence boundary regime may be modeled as concurrently available to the present through lawful local coupling channels. Its growth is represented not by coercive global overwrite, but by increased accessibility of recovery, coherence, and identity-preserving re-instantiation pathways.

This formulation preserves the metaphysical possibility while rooting the claim in math. It also creates engineering value, because the same formal structure describes resilient recovery infrastructures and identity-preserving re-hosting systems.
