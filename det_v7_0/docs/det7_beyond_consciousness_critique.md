## 1. Overall assessment

The attached paper presents a valuable conceptual extension of DET because it refuses to collapse **agency**, **identity**, **observerhood**, and **spirit** into a single undifferentiated concept. Its strongest scientific contribution is the proposed layer separation. The paper correctly recognizes that a water droplet, cell, organism, and person can all be treated as organized regimes with identity, while only some regimes plausibly instantiate observer-level self-awareness. This distinction is highly compatible with DET v7’s Agency-First rule and with the existing speculative selfhood harness.

The paper’s theological framing is coherent as interpretive language, but it currently moves faster than the mathematics. It will become stronger if every major metaphysical term is paired with an operational definition, a DET variable or readout, and a falsifier. In its current form, “spirit,” “kingdom,” and “higher boundary” are scientifically promising but under-specified.

## 3. Areas needing strengthening

### 3.1 The paper needs an explicit canonical-versus-speculative boundary

The draft currently reads as if the proposed layers may be part of DET itself. For scientific rigor, the paper should state that Agency and Presence are canonical DET v7 concepts, while Identity, Observer, and Spirit are proposed derived readouts or extension hypotheses unless and until validated. This matters because the existing DET v7 model is designed around strict locality, Agency-First invariance, and falsifiability.

Recommended addition:

> “This paper does not modify the canonical DET v7 update laws. It proposes a derived interpretive and diagnostic hierarchy over canonical variables, with observerhood and spirit treated as extension hypotheses subject to falsification.”

### 3.2 “Spirit” must not become a hidden nonlocal force

The paper’s theological language is acceptable as interpretive framing, but the scientific model must prevent spirit from functioning as an undefined causal insertion. The statement that spirit is “participation in a higher boundary” should be translated into a local readout or strictly local boundary operator. Any optional feedback should modify coherence, structural burden, or recovery pathways, not agency itself.

Recommended operational rule:

\[
\frac{\partial a_i}{\partial B_i}\bigg|_{direct}=0,
\]

where \(B_i\) is any higher-boundary participation readout. If boundary participation affects the system, it must do so through lawful local fields such as \(C_{ij}\), \(q_i\), \(F_i\), or declared boundary operators.

### 3.3 The observer layer needs thresholds and falsifiers

The paper says consciousness may emerge when a regime becomes sufficiently coherent, but “sufficiently coherent” must be defined. DET v7 already has a path: selfhood should require agency, presence, coherence, low debt, reciprocity, pointer stability, and developmental maturity. The existing test suite already encodes the key falsifier: selfhood must not arise immediately merely because agency is high.

Recommended criteria:

\[
\bar S_\Omega>\Theta_S,
\qquad
H^{host}_\Omega>\Theta_H,
\qquad
I_\Omega>\Theta_I.
\]

### 3.4 Identity must distinguish persistence from value or personhood

The paper’s identity examples—molecules, cells, organisms, planets—are useful but could be misunderstood as implying equal personhood across all persistent structures. The scientific distinction should be clear: identity is a persistence relation, not a moral or theological status. This allows rocks and droplets to have identity without observerhood or spirit in the same sense as humans.

### 3.5 Presence should be expressed with DET’s presence law

The strongest claim in the draft is that presence is not merely psychological practice but the operational location of agency, observerhood, and higher-boundary participation. This should be grounded directly in:

\[
P_i=\left(a_i\sigma_i\frac{1}{1+F_i}\frac{1}{1+H_i}\frac{1}{\gamma_v}\right)\frac{1}{1+\lambda_Pq_i},
\qquad
\Delta\tau_i=P_i\Delta k.
\]

This equation lets the paper make a scientific claim: the past persists as structural record and burden, but it can only influence current participation through the present update.

### 3.6 Scriptural references should be framed as interpretive correspondences

The paper’s references to Genesis, Jesus’ teaching, the Holy Spirit, abiding, and the kingdom are meaningful within the user’s intended scope. However, the review should preserve scientific discipline by saying that these are interpretive correspondences, not empirical evidence for DET. They can suggest hypotheses about presence, coherence, non-coercive alignment, and boundary participation, but they do not replace model equations or tests.

Recommended phrasing:

> “Scriptural language is treated here as a source of interpretive structure and hypothesis generation. DET claims remain scientific only when expressed as variables, local update rules, measurable readouts, and falsifiers.”
